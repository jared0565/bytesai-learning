# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People learning AI skills — from complete beginners exploring the field to intermediate practitioners deepening their knowledge. They land on any device: phone on a commute, tablet at home, desktop at work. Self-motivated but time-constrained; they want the right course fast, not a catalogue to scroll through. Secondary users are career-changers using BytesAI as a structured gateway into the AI field.

## Product Purpose

BytesAI Learning is a curated discovery platform for verified-free AI courses. It aggregates 100+ courses from top providers (Google, Anthropic, Stanford, DeepLearning.AI, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and 20+ others) and surfaces them through search, filtering, and personalized recommendations. Success: a user finds a relevant, high-quality course within 60 seconds of landing and trusts it is genuinely free before clicking through.

## Brand Personality

Considered, confident, human. The curation is the product — BytesAI is the trusted editor, not an aggregator. Not academic, not hustle-culture. Direct and warm without being casual.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dark-mode clones: cold blue/purple space aesthetics that say "startup landing page" instead of "trusted resource." Coursera's corporate bureaucracy. Udemy's marketplace chaos. Hero sections with gradient text and glassmorphism. The current design leans into these — the goal is to move away from them.

## Design Principles

1. **The edit is the product.** Every UI decision reinforces that BytesAI has made choices on the user's behalf. Reduce, do not aggregate.
2. **Earn trust before asking for anything.** Users should feel confident about course quality before they need to sign in or click through.
3. **Native everywhere, not just responsive.** Mobile experience is designed for mobile, not a shrunken desktop. Same principle applies to tablet and large screens — each breakpoint is a first-class layout.
4. **Light by default, dark by choice.** Editorial Precision is the primary design direction: clean, typographically strong, considered. Dark mode is a first-class variant, not an afterthought or the default.
5. **Hierarchy over decoration.** Visual weight communicates importance. No gradients, glows, or animations that do not carry meaning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Reduced motion support via prefers-reduced-motion. Sufficient contrast in both light and dark themes. Minimum 44x44px touch targets on mobile.
